AI Maturity Benchmark

An independent scoring framework ranking global financial institutions on AI readiness, deployment breadth, and innovation capability across five weighted dimensions. Scores are derived from public disclosures, earnings commentary, regulatory filings, and industry research. Benchmark is reviewed and updated quarterly.

The AI Finance Maturity Index (AFMI) scores each institution out of 100 across five equally weighted dimensions (20 points each). Scores are calibrated against peer group disclosures and third-party research; institutions receive partial credit for pilots and programmes in progress. The quarterly update cycle uses recent news coverage and published developments to assess whether scores should change; adjustments are capped at ±2 points per dimension per quarter to prevent volatility.

Strategy & Governance
Up to 20 points
Board-level AI commitment, a named Chief AI Officer or equivalent, published AI strategy, independent model risk management framework, AI ethics committee, and integration of AI into long-term capital planning.
Data & Infrastructure
Up to 20 points
Investment in cloud-native data platforms, real-time pipelines, data governance, and proprietary data assets. Leaders operate enterprise data lakes with sub-second latency serving AI workloads at scale.
Deployment at Scale
Up to 20 points
Number and breadth of live AI use cases across business lines. Partial credit for supervised pilots; full credit requires production deployments with measurable outcomes.
Talent & Investment
Up to 20 points
Disclosed AI/ML headcount, R&D spend as a proportion of revenue, structured upskilling programmes, and retention of senior AI talent. Scores normalised for institution size.
Innovation & IP
Up to 20 points
AI-related patent filings, development of proprietary foundation models, peer-reviewed research, and participation in standards bodies and industry consortia (e.g., Partnership on AI, BIS Innovation Hub).
Maturity Tiers
Leader 85–100 · Enterprise-wide AI; significant proprietary model development
Advanced 65–84 · Broad deployment; strong governance; active investment
Developing 50–64 · Meaningful AI in select functions; governance maturing
Emerging 0–49 · Early-stage deployment; foundational infrastructure underway

Disclaimer: Scores are indicative assessments for informational purposes only and are not credit ratings, investment recommendations, or regulatory determinations. AI programmes evolve rapidly; scores reflect the state of public information as of the last review date. Institutions may score differently on proprietary or internal assessments.
