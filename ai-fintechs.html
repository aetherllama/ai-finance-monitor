AI FinTech Performance Index (AFPI)

The AFPI evaluates AI-native FinTech companies across five equally-weighted dimensions (20 pts each, 100 pts total), focusing on the strength of the AI solution and its depth of adoption within regulated financial institutions.

Scope: AI-native FinTechs and AI-first divisions of financial technology vendors where machine learning is core to the product — not peripheral. Traditional software vendors with AI add-ons are excluded.

Scoring Tiers

Elite 80–100 · Category-defining with global Tier-1 FI deployment
Leader 65–79 · Strong institutional adoption across multiple product lines
Growth 50–64 · Validated solutions gaining enterprise traction
Emerging 0–49 · Early-stage deployment, product-market fit forming
1
AI Innovation / 20
Sophistication, novelty, and production depth of AI capabilities — proprietary models, research pedigree, technical differentiation from rule-based alternatives. Frontier AI (graph neural networks, LLMs, adaptive ML) deployed at scale scores near 20.
2
FI Client Depth / 20
Breadth and quality of financial institution clients in production — number of banks, asset managers, insurers, and payment processors; client tier (G-SIBs score higher than community banks); and integration depth (core infrastructure vs. peripheral tooling).
3
Processing Scale / 20
Operational scale: transaction volumes processed, assets under analysis, data volume handled daily. Solutions processing trillions in transactions score near 20; early-stage platforms with limited throughput score below 10.
4
Global Reach / 20
International footprint: regulatory jurisdictions covered, multi-currency and multi-language support, and presence across mature markets (US, UK, EU) and high-growth regions (APAC, Middle East, LatAm).
5
Commercial Traction / 20
Financial health and market validation: ARR growth trajectory, funding rounds and valuations, strategic acquisitions, major FI partnerships, and public market performance where applicable.

Scores are based on publicly available information: press releases, funding announcements, company disclosures, and AI capability assessments. Updated quarterly. v1.0 — Last reviewed 2026-05-20.
