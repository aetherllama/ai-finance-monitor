The AI FinTech Performance Index (AFPI) scores each company out of 100 across five equally weighted dimensions (20 points each). The index focuses specifically on the strength of the company's AI technology and its depth of production adoption within regulated financial institutions — banks, asset managers, insurers, and payment processors. Companies must have AI as a core product capability (not a feature add-on) to qualify. Scores are calibrated against peer disclosures, funding announcements, client references, and product capability assessments.

AI Innovation
Up to 20 points
Sophistication, novelty, and production depth of AI capabilities — proprietary model development, research pedigree, patent activity, and technical differentiation from rule-based alternatives. Frontier AI (graph neural networks, LLMs, adaptive behavioral ML) deployed at scale scores near 20.
FI Client Depth
Up to 20 points
Breadth and quality of financial institution clients in production — number of banks, asset managers, insurers, and payment processors; client tier (G-SIBs score higher than community banks); and integration depth (core infrastructure vs. peripheral tooling).
Processing Scale
Up to 20 points
Operational scale of the deployed solution: transaction volumes processed, assets under analysis, or data volume handled daily. Solutions processing trillions in transactions score near 20; early-stage platforms with limited throughput score below 10.
Global Reach
Up to 20 points
International deployment footprint: number of regulatory jurisdictions covered, multi-currency and multi-language support, and presence across both mature markets (US, UK, EU) and high-growth regions (APAC, Middle East, LatAm).
Commercial Traction
Up to 20 points
Financial health and market validation: ARR growth trajectory, funding rounds and valuations, strategic acquisitions by major FIs or tech firms, flagship FI partnerships, and public market performance where applicable.
AFPI Tiers
Elite 80–100 · Category-defining platform with global Tier-1 FI deployment
Leader 65–79 · Strong institutional adoption across multiple product lines
Growth 50–64 · Validated solution gaining enterprise traction
Emerging 0–49 · Early-stage deployment; product-market fit forming

Disclaimer: Scores are indicative assessments based on publicly available information — press releases, funding announcements, company disclosures, and product capability assessments. They are not investment recommendations, credit ratings, or regulatory determinations. AI capabilities evolve rapidly; scores reflect the state of public information as of the last review date.
